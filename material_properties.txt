WAVEGUIDE SIMULATOR — MATERIAL PROPERTY REFERENCE
=================================================
These are the exact default values used in waveguide_simulator.html (Materials
and Compare tabs). Full source citations for every value are in the tool's
References tab. Prices are approximate market figures and move over time;
embodied-carbon (CO2e) figures are cradle-to-gate ballpark.

Units:  sigma = electrical conductivity [S/m]
        density [kg/m^3]
        price  [USD per kg, approximate]
        eps_r' / eps_r'' = real / imaginary relative permittivity
        mu_r'  / mu_r''  = real / imaginary relative permeability
        CO2e   [kg CO2e per kg, cradle-to-gate, ballpark]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IN THE SIMULATOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Materials tab -> Test material -> "Coating on substrate":
    Coating   : take sigma, density, price, CO2e from the CONDUCTORS table
    Substrate : take eps_r', density, price, CO2e from the SUBSTRATES table
Materials tab -> Test material -> "Solid block":
    take sigma, density, price from the CONDUCTORS table (e.g. solid silver)
A "Quick pick" dropdown loads any preset candidate above.
Every value here is cited in the tool's References tab.
